Sound Matching with a Differentiable Karplus-Strong Algorithm

Pablo Tablas de Paula, David Marttila, Rodrigo Díaz, Irán Román, Emmanouil Benetos, Joshua D Reiss
Centre For Digital Music C4DM, Queen Mary University Of London
Code Repository

Abstract

We present an event-based sound matching model using a differentiable extended KSA. To avoid relying on external onset and fundamental frequency (\(f_0\)) detectors, we explore training methodologies combining parameter losses (\(\mathcal{P}_{\text{loss}}\)) on synthetic data with audio losses. We demonstrate that time-domain fractional delay interpolation provides gradient accuracy comparable to frequency-sampling while avoiding time-aliasing in time-varying scenarios. Through systematic gradient analysis, we reveal that standard spectral losses provide no meaningful directional gradients for onset times, heavily degrading joint training. Training exclusively with \(\mathcal{P}_{\text{loss}}\) on synthetic data effectively learns \(f_0\), timbral parameters, and onset times, but struggles to generalise to real-world recordings due to domain shifts in transient features. External detectors combined with audio losses generalise best, isolating the model to timbre optimisation. While our KSA decoder achieves lower sample-level reconstruction than HN baselines, it reaches comparable semantic reconstruction for plucked instruments, better capturing higher-level timbral features.
